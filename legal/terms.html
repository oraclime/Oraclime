1. About ORACLIME

ORACLIME coordinates service requirements between customers, businesses, partners and technicians. The exact service provider, scope of work, price, schedule and applicable warranty should be confirmed before work begins.

2. Booking and Service Scope

Customers should provide accurate contact details, service address, appliance details and a clear description of the issue. Any inspection, repair, installation, spare part or additional charge must be explained and agreed before the work proceeds.

3. Pricing and Payment

Charges depend on the confirmed service, location, labour and parts. Ask for a price breakdown before approving paid work. Do not assume a quote includes spare parts, transportation or additional repairs unless stated in writing.

4. Warranty and Service Support

ORACLIME's service workmanship warranty, when applicable, is separate from a manufacturer's product warranty. Warranty duration, coverage, exclusions and claim process must be stated on the job invoice or service record. Manufacturer warranty is subject to the relevant brand's official terms.

5. Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide safe and reasonable access to the service location.
  • Share correct appliance and purchase/warranty information when relevant.
  • Review and approve the quoted scope before work starts.
  • Keep the invoice or job ID for support requests.

Appointment times may change due to availability, location, parts or circumstances outside reasonable control. Any applicable cancellation or visit charge should be communicated before confirmation.
