Anti-discrimination Policy

ORACLIME aims to support fair and respectful interactions among customers, technicians, partners, business clients and staff involved in service coordination.

Important: This page describes ORACLIME's intended operating terms. Publish only after checking that the policies match your actual business practices, service records, and applicable law.

1. Our standard

People should be treated respectfully and service decisions should be based on relevant factors such as the type of job, skills, safety, availability, service area, price, and operational requirements—not irrelevant personal characteristics.

2. Expected behaviour

  • Communicate politely and professionally.
  • Do not harass, threaten, intimidate, or use discriminatory slurs.
  • Do not refuse service or allocate work on an unlawful discriminatory basis.
  • Respect personal boundaries, privacy, property, and safety.
  • Raise concerns honestly and provide relevant details for review.

3. Customers and service professionals

Customers should be able to raise service concerns without harassment. Technicians and partners should be assessed on job-relevant competence, reliability, availability, safety, and agreed performance standards. ORACLIME may take reasonable steps to protect a person from unsafe or abusive conduct, subject to applicable law and the facts of the situation.

4. Reporting a concern

Contact ORACLIME by WhatsApp or email using the details below. Include the job reference, date, people involved, what happened, and any relevant messages or documents. Avoid sharing unrelated sensitive personal information. If there is an immediate threat to safety, contact the appropriate emergency authorities first.

5. Review and action

ORACLIME will review concerns reasonably and, where appropriate, seek information from relevant parties. Depending on the facts, possible actions may include clarification, guidance, changes to assignment or coordination, restriction of platform access, or referral to the appropriate authority. Outcomes depend on the circumstances and applicable law; confidentiality cannot be promised where disclosure is necessary for a fair review or legally required.

6. No retaliation

Retaliation for raising a genuine concern in good faith is not acceptable. Knowingly false or abusive complaints may be reviewed appropriately, but a complaint should not be dismissed merely because it cannot be substantiated.

7. Policy scope

This policy describes ORACLIME's intended standards for its service-coordination activities. It does not replace applicable employment, equality, accessibility, consumer-protection, or other legal requirements.
