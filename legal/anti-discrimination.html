Anti-discrimination Policy

We aim to treat customers, technicians, partners and colleagues fairly and respectfully.

1. Equal and Respectful Treatment

ORACLIME expects professional, respectful conduct throughout service coordination. People should not be denied ordinary service consideration or treated unfairly on the basis of personal characteristics protected by applicable law.

2. Expected Conduct

  • Communicate politely and professionally.
  • Make service decisions using relevant factors such as skills, availability, location, safety and service requirements.
  • Do not use abusive, threatening, harassing or discriminatory language.
  • Respect customer and technician privacy.

3. Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness discriminatory conduct related to an ORACLIME-coordinated service, share the job ID, date and a description of the concern. Do not send unnecessary sensitive personal information.

4. Review

ORACLIME will review reported concerns and take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances and applicable law. Outcomes may depend on the evidence available.
