Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ORACLIME may handle personal information when you visit the website, contact support, or request a coordinated service.

Important: This page describes ORACLIME's intended operating terms. Publish only after checking that the policies match your actual business practices, service records, and applicable law.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address and service address.
  • Service details: appliance type, brand/model, reported fault, appointment preferences, photographs shared for diagnosis, job status, invoice and service history.
  • Communication records: messages, support requests, complaint details and feedback.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, log or usage information that may be generated when the website is accessed, depending on the hosting and tools in use.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, government identity documents, or other sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary and a secure, appropriate method has been provided.

2. How information is used

  • Respond to enquiries and create or manage service requests.
  • Coordinate appointments, technicians, business partners and service completion.
  • Share job details with the assigned technician or partner to the extent needed to perform the service.
  • Maintain service records, process payments or invoices where applicable, and handle complaints or warranty claims.
  • Protect the website and users, prevent misuse, meet legal obligations, and maintain business records.

3. Sharing information

Information may be shared on a need-to-know basis with the technician or partner assigned to a job, relevant business customer, hosting or communication providers, payment providers if used, professional advisers, or authorities where required by law. ORACLIME should not sell personal information as a business practice. Recipients may process information under their own legal obligations and privacy practices.

4. Consent and choice

By voluntarily submitting information, you ask ORACLIME to use the relevant details to respond to your enquiry or coordinate the requested service. Where consent is legally required, it should be obtained separately and can be withdrawn subject to legal or contractual limits. You may choose not to provide optional information, though the requested service may then be harder or impossible to coordinate.

5. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, service records, warranty/complaint handling, accounting, security, or legal requirements. ORACLIME should apply reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No website, messaging channel, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Cookies and third-party services

The website may use essential technical storage or third-party services depending on its actual configuration and hosting. This policy should be updated if analytics, advertising, embedded media, forms, maps, or other tracking tools are added. Please check your browser settings for cookie controls.

7. Your requests and grievances

You may contact ORACLIME to ask about personal information associated with your service request, request correction of inaccurate details, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records where required by law or needed to resolve a claim.

8. Children and third-party details

The website is not intended to invite children to provide personal information independently. If you provide another person's contact details, ensure you are authorised to share them for the service purpose. Notify ORACLIME if information was submitted in error.

9. Policy updates

This policy may be revised when the website, service workflow, or legal requirements change. The published version should include an effective date. If the policy changes materially, ORACLIME should provide notice where required by applicable law.
