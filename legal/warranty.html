Warranty & Support

Warranty coverage depends on the specific service, the invoice or service record, the parts supplied, and the applicable manufacturer's terms. Check the written documents for your job.

Important: This page describes ORACLIME's intended operating terms. Publish only after checking that the policies match your actual business practices, service records, and applicable law.

1. ORACLIME service warranty

Where expressly confirmed in the invoice, work order, or warranty record, eligible repair/workmanship services may carry a 3-month service warranty from the date stated in that record. Do not assume that every visit, inspection, installation, cleaning, maintenance job, or part is automatically covered.

What the service warranty may cover

Only the repair or workmanship specifically described in the service record and confirmed as covered. The record should identify the covered work, start date, duration, and how to raise a claim.

What may not be covered

  • A different or unrelated fault.
  • Damage caused by misuse, accident, pests, voltage issues, water ingress, external causes, or unauthorised repair, where the written warranty terms lawfully exclude it.
  • Consumables, replacement parts, or manufacturer-controlled items unless expressly included in writing.
  • Normal wear and tear or a fault that could not reasonably have been identified during the original work, subject to the actual terms and applicable law.

Any exclusion must be clearly stated in the applicable warranty terms and cannot remove rights that the law does not allow a business to exclude.

2. Manufacturer warranty — Voltas, Lloyd, Blue Star and other brands

For air conditioners and other branded products, the manufacturer's product warranty is governed by that brand's warranty card, registration, invoice, product model, installation requirements, and official terms. Coverage and duration vary by model, component, purchase date and manufacturer policy.

ORACLIME does not claim to be an authorised service centre or representative of Voltas, Lloyd, Blue Star, or any other brand unless a current written authorisation specifically confirms that status. A service arranged through ORACLIME does not automatically extend or replace the manufacturer's warranty. Customers should check directly with the brand for official warranty eligibility and authorised service options.

3. Long-term support

ORACLIME may assist with service-history questions and support enquiries for up to 10 years where records remain available and the business is able to assist. This is a support-intention statement, not a guarantee of free repairs, free parts, product life, parts availability, or a 10-year repair warranty. The exact assistance available depends on the job record, operating capacity, and applicable law.

4. How to request warranty support

  1. Keep the invoice, booking/job ID, service date, and warranty record.
  2. Contact ORACLIME using the details below and describe the issue.
  3. Share relevant photographs or a short video if requested and safe to do so.
  4. Do not arrange unrelated or unauthorised work before contacting support if you want the original workmanship reviewed, unless urgent safety measures are needed.
  5. ORACLIME may ask to inspect the issue or arrange a technician assessment before deciding whether the reported fault falls within the written coverage.

A request is not approved merely because it has been submitted. ORACLIME should communicate the review outcome and any applicable charges before performing chargeable work.

5. Response time and service availability

WhatsApp support is available for enquiries 24/7. This means messages may be submitted at any time; it does not guarantee an immediate response, a technician visit at all hours, same-day resolution, or availability of parts. Any appointment or response commitment must be confirmed separately.

6. Consumer rights

This page does not limit any mandatory rights available under applicable consumer-protection or other law. If a written invoice, job-specific warranty, or manufacturer's terms provide a more specific applicable commitment, review those documents alongside this page.
