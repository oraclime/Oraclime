Warranty & 10-Year Support

Understand the difference between ORACLIME service workmanship coverage and a manufacturer's product warranty.

ORACLIME 3-Month Service Workmanship Warranty

3 Months

ORACLIME Service Warranty

ORACLIME proposes a 3-month workmanship warranty for eligible repair work carried out by an ORACLIME-assigned technician, starting from the service completion date.

Up to 10 Years

Support Commitment

ORACLIME aims to provide service-record assistance and help customers contact an appropriate service channel for up to 10 years, subject to service availability and retained records. This is not a promise of free repairs, free parts or guaranteed technician availability.

What the 3-month warranty may cover

  • Repeat occurrence of the same fault where it is directly caused by the covered repair workmanship.
  • Assessment of the original job record and repair performed.

What is not automatically covered

  • New or unrelated faults, physical damage, misuse, electrical supply issues, water/fire damage or external causes.
  • Consumables, spare parts or components unless the job invoice explicitly includes them.
  • Work changed or repaired by another provider after the original service, where it affects the claimed issue.
  • Manufacturer product defects or claims that must be handled under the brand's warranty.

Before publishing: Only advertise the 3-month warranty if ORACLIME is ready to honour it operationally and the terms are approved. Define the claim process, exclusions and any visit/part charges on the invoice. The 10-year support wording is assistance only, not a 10-year repair warranty.

Manufacturer Warranty — Voltas, Lloyd, Blue Star and Other AC Brands

Air-conditioner manufacturer warranties are governed by the brand's own warranty card, invoice, model, product registration and official policy. Terms can differ by model, component, installation conditions and purchase date.

For Voltas, Lloyd, Blue Star or another brand, customers should check the official warranty documents or contact the brand's authorized support channel. ORACLIME does not claim to extend or replace a manufacturer's warranty and does not represent itself as brand-authorized unless expressly stated.

Important distinction

  • ORACLIME warranty: applies only to eligible workmanship explicitly covered by the ORACLIME job record.
  • Manufacturer warranty: applies only as specified by the brand's own policy.
  • 10-year support: assistance with service history and finding a support route where possible; not a guarantee of parts, labour, free service or continued operation.
