About ORACLIME

Connecting Business Requirements With Reliable Service Execution.

ORACLIME is a B2B and Business-to-Technician service coordination platform focused on turning service requirements into professionally managed customer service operations.

Who We Are

ORACLIME works with businesses, companies, contractors, dealers and other organizations to generate and coordinate service leads. We also work directly with technicians and service professionals to execute those requirements at the customer location.

Our role is to connect the business requirement with the right service professional and coordinate the process until the service is completed and properly documented.

What We Do

Lead Generation

Receive and organize service requirements.

B2B Partnerships

Build repeat relationships with businesses and organizations.

B2T Coordination

Connect requirements with suitable technicians.

Customer Service

Coordinate execution, verification and closure.

Our Operating Model

Businesses & Partners

Submit service requirements to ORACLIME.

ORACLIME Operations

Review, manage and assign work based on capability, availability, service area and operational requirements.

Technicians

Receive assigned work through a mobile-friendly workflow and execute service at the customer location.

Why It Matters

For Businesses / Partners

  • Structured lead tracking
  • Technician coordination
  • Better visibility
  • Completion records

For Technicians

  • Organized service leads
  • Clear job information

  • Service history
